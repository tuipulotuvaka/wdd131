1. Site Name

Discover Tonga

Logo/Brand Name: Discover Tonga

I chose the name Discover Tonga because the website helps visitors discover the beauty, culture, traditions, food, history, beaches, and activities of Tonga. The name is simple, memorable, and clearly represents the purpose of the website.

Suggested domain: discovertonga.org

2. Site Purpose

The purpose of Discover Tonga is to provide useful and interesting information about Tonga and its islands. The website introduces visitors to Tongan culture, traditional food, beaches, history, community life, and activities that can be experienced in Tonga.

The website provides information and visual content that helps visitors learn about Tonga and discover places and experiences they may want to explore. A contact page will also allow visitors to share their favorite Tongan place, food, activity, or cultural experience.

3. Scenarios

The following questions represent information that members of the target audience may want to find when visiting the website:

  • What are some beaches and island activities I can experience when visiting Tonga?
  • What traditional foods and cultural traditions can I learn about in Tonga?
  • What can I learn about Tongan history and community life?
  • How can I share my favorite Tongan place, food, or activity with the website?

4. Color Schema

The color scheme is inspired by the Pacific Ocean, the natural environment of Tonga, and warm tropical colors. These colors will be used throughout the website to create a consistent visual identity.

Primary Blue #003B5C Headers, navigation, headings, and footer.
Secondary Blue #005F73 Secondary headings, buttons, and accents.
Golden Accent #EE9B00 Highlights, borders, and important accents.
Light Background #F4F7F8 Main page background and content areas.
Color usage: Dark blue colors will provide strong contrast for headings and navigation. White will be used with dark backgrounds, while the golden accent will draw attention to important elements.

5. Typography

The website will use Arial as its main font. Arial is clean, readable, and available across many different devices.

Arial — Headings

Arial will be used for page headings, section headings, navigation, and important titles.

Arial — Body Text

Arial will be used for paragraphs, descriptions, lists, forms, and general website content.

Font family: Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif

Responsive design: On smaller screens, the navigation will use a menu button and content will be displayed in a single-column layout. On wider screens, the navigation and content will use more horizontal space and multiple content areas can appear side by side.

7. Development Plan

The final Discover Tonga website will use HTML for structure, CSS for responsive styling, and JavaScript for interactive features and dynamically generated content.

  • Home: Introduce Tonga and highlight major experiences.
  • Explore: Provide information about beaches, culture, food, activities, history, and community.
  • Contact: Allow visitors to submit their favorite Tongan place, food, activity, or experience.
  • References: List information and image sources used to create the website.
