About Tonga

A special island destination

Tonga is a Polynesian kingdom made up of many islands in the South Pacific. Its natural beauty and strong cultural traditions make it a unique destination.

Whether you are interested in beaches, history, food, nature, or local culture, Tonga has something interesting to discover.
